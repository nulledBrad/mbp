## Definition
A fee the franchisee pays the franchisor for the right to enter a franchise agreement, giving the right to sell goods under the franchisor's trademarks and access to its business processes ([Franchise fee — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchise_fee)). Ongoing payments typically include a trademark royalty and a percentage of unit sales ([Franchising — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchising)).

## Business context
Royalties tied to outlet sales make franchise revenue a direct driver of franchisor income.
