## Definition
"Any person who grants a franchise and participates in the franchise relationship" ([16 CFR 436.1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-436)). The franchisor licenses its business model, know-how and brand to franchisees ([Franchising — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchising)).

## Business context
The franchisor monitors network performance, approves suppliers and compares outlets. It is the main audience for network-level KPIs.

## Data usage
Not a column: the franchisor is the owner of the whole dataset. Network-level measures aggregate over all `franchiseID`s.
