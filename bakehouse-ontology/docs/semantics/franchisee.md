## Definition
"Any person who is granted a franchise" ([16 CFR 436.1](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-D/part-436)). The franchisee pays fees to the franchisor and agrees to comply with its obligations ([Franchising — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franchising)).

## Business context
Franchisees run the outlets and are accountable for outlet sales and customer experience.

Not yet mapped: there is no franchisee or owner column. `sales_franchises.name` names the outlet, not its owner.
