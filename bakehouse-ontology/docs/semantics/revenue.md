## Definition
Revenue is the total income generated by the sale of goods and services related to a business's primary operations, without taking expenses into account; it is also called sales or turnover ([Revenue — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenue)).

## Business context
The headline measure of franchise and network performance. Franchisors and franchisees track it by outlet, product, payment method and day, and it is the base for average transaction value, revenue per franchise and same-store sales.

## Data usage
`SUM(totalPrice)` over `samples.bakehouse.sales_transactions`, at transaction-row grain, rolled up by `franchiseID`, `product`, `paymentMethod` or `dateTime`. This is gross sales: there are no discount, tax, refund or cost columns. The currency is not stated.
