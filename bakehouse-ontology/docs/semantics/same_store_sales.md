## Definition
The difference in revenue generated by a retail chain's existing outlets over a period, compared to an identical period in the past, usually the previous year. New and closed outlets are excluded so the comparison is like for like ([Same-store sales — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-store_sales)).

## Business context
Separates organic outlet growth from growth that comes from opening new franchises.

## Data usage
Growth = (current-period `SUM(totalPrice)` − prior-period `SUM(totalPrice)`) / prior-period `SUM(totalPrice)`, restricted to `franchiseID`s with sales in both periods, from `samples.bakehouse.sales_transactions`. The data covers about 17 days, so only short periods (week over week) are possible. Year over year is not yet mapped.
