## Definition
A loyalty score that groups survey respondents into promoters (rating 9 or 10), passives (7 or 8) and detractors (6 or lower), and subtracts the percentage of detractors from the percentage of promoters ([Net promoter score — Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_promoter_score)).

## Business context
A common loyalty benchmark that stakeholders may ask for. In this dataset it cannot be measured directly.

## Data usage
Not yet mapped: there is no 0-10 rating column. Do not approximate NPS from review sentiment. Answer NPS questions with review sentiment and say that NPS is not available.
