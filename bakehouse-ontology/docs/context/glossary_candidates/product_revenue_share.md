## Definition
Product revenue share is a product's revenue divided by total revenue in the same filter context.

## Business context
Franchise operations managers use it to understand product mix at a franchise or across the network.

## Data usage
Revenue for the product divided by revenue across all products with the same filters applied (period, franchise, country). Implementation needs review in metric view synthesis.
